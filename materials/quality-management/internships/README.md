# Internship projects — Quality Management

Internships for students of the BSc in Engineering Management (L-8), built on the measurement part of the course. Each project has its own folder, with the plan and everything needed to start.

| Project | Students | In short |
|---|---|---|
| [How well does a camera measure displacement?](pyidi-displacement/) | two, one track each | Simplified Optical Flow and Lucas–Kanade in pyIDI: settings, speckle size and motion magnitude, studied on synthetic images with a known displacement, then tested on real measurements |

Interested in an internship like these? Open an [issue](../../../../../issues) in this repository, or ask after a lecture.
